# Contributing to Awesome AI Cost Optimization

Thanks for contributing. The goal is simple: help teams spend less on AI without getting less from AI.

## What belongs here

- Tools that help measure, reduce, or optimize AI spend
- Techniques with real-world cost impact (not theoretical)
- Benchmarks and pricing comparisons with actual numbers
- Case studies with before/after cost data
- Open source alternatives to expensive hosted services

## What doesn't belong here

- Vendor marketing pages or press releases
- Generic "AI tips" articles without cost data
- Tools that don't directly relate to cost optimization
- Paywalled content (resources should be accessible)

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repo
2. **Adding your own tool or product?** Add it to the **Community Tools** section in README.md — that's where new submissions land. Entries get promoted into the curated sections once they prove out (real adoption, active maintenance, verifiable numbers).
3. Adding a third-party resource (article, case study, benchmark, established OSS tool)? Add it to the appropriate curated section.
4. Follow the existing format: `[Resource Name](URL) - One-line description`
5. Submit a PR with a brief explanation of why this resource helps reduce AI costs

## Format

```markdown
- [Tool Name](https://example.com) - What it does and why it saves money
```

Keep descriptions under 20 words. Let the link do the talking.

## Quality bar

Ask yourself: "Would this help an engineering lead cut their AI bill this week?" If yes, submit it. If it's theoretical or aspirational, skip it.
